# Reference pass: what PACT borrows and what it avoids

Inspected 2026-10-05 in the built-in browser. These are public marketing pages, not signed-in products, so the notes are about
composition and tone, not interaction detail. What each page let me see is stated first.

## Partiful (partiful.com): fully visible
Seen: a hero of real people mid-laugh, then an event card (big title, date and place as plain lines, "Hosted by", a guest list of
overlapping faces with "+9", and three round answer buttons: Going, Maybe, Can't go). Feature copy: "Stalk the guest list", "Find a time
that works" (a poll before a date), "Send invites on any platform" (WhatsApp is named).
- **Borrow:** people are the content, not decoration (faces are on the card, not behind a tab); the answer is a one-tap, three-way
  control on the thing itself; the thing has a strong single identity (one title, one date, one place); sentences about people
  ("who is going") instead of counters.
- **Avoid:** party decoration (animated backgrounds, poster art, sparkle emoji), template-picker chrome, loudness. PACT's friends
  plan dinners, trips and bills as much as parties.

## Linear (linear.app): partly visible
Seen: near-black page, the hero art had not rendered when captured, then a calm statement in large type and a logo row.
- **Borrow:** restraint. One weight of emphasis per block, hairlines instead of boxes, a confident type scale, state shown by a small
  mark (a dot, a checkmark) instead of a coloured card.
- **Avoid:** enterprise density, dark-first chrome, project-management vocabulary, tables.

## Airbnb (airbnb.com): fully visible
Seen: a quiet white page of large rounded photographs with a single caption line each, "Guest favourite" as a small chip, one price
per item, and a one-time modal explaining all-in pricing.
- **Borrow:** warmth through generous whitespace and soft radii; one headline fact per card; a place or a person as the visual lead;
  saying the honest total up front.
- **Avoid:** photography we don't have, search-first structure, heavy shadows.

## Notion Calendar (notion.com/product/calendar): visible. Amie (amie.so): partly
Notion Calendar: a very plain "It's time." headline over a calendar with a mini month and coloured event blocks. Amie now markets an AI
note taker, so its calendar identity was not on the page I could see; treat Amie as unverified and lean on Notion Calendar.
- **Borrow:** the date as the anchor (big numeral, small weekday), time shown as rhythm (a range, a countdown) rather than a grid,
  low chrome around the schedule.
- **Avoid:** the calendar grid, hour columns, colour-per-calendar legends.

## Cash App / Revolut: Revolut visible, Cash App not
Cash App answered "not available in your region", so nothing was inspected there. Revolut: a large amount (£6,012) set over a human
photograph with one small "Accounts" pill.
- **Borrow (Revolut):** the amount is the biggest thing on a money surface and sits on a person, not a dashboard; one small pill for the
  one action; confident whole-number type.
- **Avoid:** wallet-first structure, account lists, balance cards as the home of the app. (Split and Pact are not in this phase.)

## What this means for the four screens in this phase
- **Home:** one lead moment, everything else quieter. A one-line pulse says what is waiting and what is next. Time is shown as dates.
- **Circle:** a place with people in it. Names in a sentence, larger faces, one clear Invite. Things happening are flat rows, not a stack of tinted cards.
- **Plan:** time becoming real. A big date, the span and the countdown, who is in as a sentence, the answer under your thumb.
- **Me:** your history with people, not an account page. Verification and sign-in details live in Settings.
