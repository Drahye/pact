## A. Executive assessment

**Strong.** The foundations are better than the feature count suggests. Config refuses to boot production with dev secrets, sandbox payments, or exposed OTP codes. Sessions are short JWTs bound to a revocable session row, with rotating refresh tokens and theft detection. OTPs are keyed-hashed, single purpose, attempt-limited. Ledger entries are balanced by a trigger and immutable; audit log is immutable; webhooks are signature-checked. The V2 objects have server-side authorization on every route, 256-bit share tokens, row-level security on reads, and a production-role test run that passes. Money is integer kobo end to end; split arithmetic is exact. Analytics is allow-listed and pseudonymous, with tests proving it.

**Fragile.** State that has to agree across objects: a Plan can be left permanently "became a Pact" after its Pact is cancelled; Home does not refresh after the actions that clear its cards; V2 create calls are not idempotent; closing an account does not touch V2 data. The client polls aggressively and ships one 1 MB main chunk.

**Dangerous (not P0, but fix before strangers get links).** Every share token and Circle invite token is written to the request log in full. Any screen wider than 600 px shows a fake phone frame with a demo-accounts panel ("Demo PIN 1357") regardless of environment.

**Confusing.** The mental model is not taught anywhere: onboarding, landing, page titles, manifest and the first-time Home still describe a single money-goal Pact. The Activity tab shows Pact activity only, while Home's Recent links to it. Back goes to a hard-coded parent, not where you came from. Nothing can be started without first having a Circle.

**Before broader beta.** Batch 1 and the first half of Batch 2 in section 74: about 15 changes, one small migration.

No P0 was found in what I inspected. That is not a claim that none exists in the unsampled money paths.

## B. Architecture map

**Frontend.** React 18, Vite, react-router 7. One `AppShell` wraps `/app/*`; public share routes (`/a`, `/p`, `/s`, `/r`) are lazy and sit inside the same shell. 9 `lazy()` imports in `App.tsx`; every authenticated screen is in the main chunk. Data: TanStack Query, `staleTime` 5 s default, polling 8 s on Ask/Plan/Split, 15-20 s elsewhere, no optimistic updates, `mutations.retry=false`. Auth state in an `AuthProvider`; access token in memory, refresh token in an httpOnly cookie. Forms are local state, no drafts. Sheets use one `Modal` (role=dialog, aria-modal, focus handling). Motion: framer-motion with `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`, GSAP only on marketing/hold-button/install. Push: opt-in service worker that does no caching. Analytics: `POST /me/onboarding-event` for client events, everything else recorded server-side. Errors: `ApiError` with code and message, shared `ErrorState` / `Notice`.

**Backend.** Fastify 5; `buildApp` registers helmet, CORS, cookies, rate limit (Postgres counters), error handler, `/api` routes (public `api`, authenticated `priv`). Modules per domain (`circles`, `asks`, `plans`, `splits`, `home`, `pacts`, `pactMoney`, `wallet`, `ledger`, `conversation`, `plan` (Pact tasks/budget), `push`, `events`). Authorization is in the module (V2) or by RLS via `ctx.db.asUser` (original Pact reads). Transactions via `ctx.db.tx`, row locks (`FOR UPDATE`) on the object being changed. A job outbox + worker sends push and runs deadline jobs. Webhook route takes raw body and verifies the signature. 19 migrations before V2; V2 adds 020-027.

## D. P1 findings

### SEC-1: Share tokens and invite tokens are logged in full (Security, Infrastructure)
- **Files:** `server/src/app.ts` (logger config, around lines 78-90).
- **Evidence:** Fastify's default request log includes `req.url`. I ran the server at `LOG_LEVEL=info` and requested `/api/split-links/AAAA…`; the log shows `"url": "/api/split-links/AAAA…"`. `redact.paths` covers headers and body fields but not the URL. This applies to `/api/circle-invites/:token`, `/ask-links/`, `/plan-links/`, `/split-links/`, `/recap-links/`, `/api/invites/:code` and the static `/a/ /p/ /s/ /r/` routes. The Circle invite token is a join capability.
- **Impact:** anyone with log access (hosting dashboard, log shipper, support) can join Circles and see the private previews. Logs are usually retained and shared more widely than the database.
- **Fix:** add a `req` serializer (or `redact` on `req.url`) that replaces the last path segment of those routes with `[token]`. About 10 lines plus one test.
- **Gate:** public sandbox beta. Acceptable for a trusted sandbox beta if logs are private.

### DATA-1: Account closure and data export ignore everything built since Pacts (Data, Privacy)
- **Files:** `server/src/modules/users.ts` `closeAccount` (line ~160), `exportData` (line ~124); `server/src/modules/push.ts`.
- **Evidence:** `closeAccount` blockers and cleanup mention wallet, Pacts, withdrawals, bank accounts and notifications only. It does not: delete `push_subscriptions` (push delivery selects by `user_id` with no status check, push.ts:97); leave Circles (the closed user stays a `joined` member, shown as "Former member", and still counts toward the member cap; if they own a Circle it is orphaned); check open Splits where they are payer or owe a share; check Plans they organise. `exportData` returns profile, Pacts, wallet, banks, sessions, notifications, and none of: Circles, answers, RSVPs, Plans, Splits and shares, comments you made in V2.
- **Impact:** a closed account's browser can keep receiving pushes; open Splits can be owed to or by a ghost forever; the data-controls promise (export "your data") is untrue for most of what the product now holds. Splits include debts, which are the most sensitive V2 data.
- **Fix:** on closure: delete push subscriptions, set Circle membership to `left` (block or transfer if sole owner with other members, as `leaveCircle` already does), and block closure while the user is payer or owes an open Split share. Add V2 sections to the export. Add a test per rule.
- **Gate:** public sandbox beta (privacy promise). Trusted beta: document it.

### LIFE-1: A Plan can be stranded as "became a Pact" when the Pact never completes (Product, Data)
- **Files:** `server/src/modules/plans.ts` `setStatus` (cancel blocked when `pact_id`), `completeFromPact`; `server/src/modules/pacts.ts` `cancel` (line ~1094) and the missed-deadline job (~1190).
- **Evidence:** after conversion, the Plan cannot be cancelled (`plan_has_pact`) or marked done by hand (`plan_completes_via_pact`), and only `completePact` moves it to done. `cancel`, refunds, and the deadline job never touch `plans`. `grep plan_id|plans` in `pacts.ts` finds only the create-time link.
- **Impact:** a cancelled or refunded Pact leaves a Plan that says "The group is now committing and executing the plan through its Pact", with no actions, forever; it also keeps `plan_has_pact` so it can never be cancelled or converted again. Home hides it from Coming up (pact_id set) so it quietly disappears from the places people would notice it.
- **Fix:** when a linked Pact is cancelled/refunded/released without completion, either cancel the Plan or clear `pact_id` and log "The Pact was cancelled" so the Plan is editable again. Pick one; I recommend clearing the link and logging it (history is kept, the group can try again). One transaction in each place; two tests.
- **Gate:** trusted beta (it will be hit by the second group that cancels a Pact).

### PROD-1: Every screen wider than 600 px is a fake phone with a demo panel (Product, UI, Security)
- **Files:** `src/pages/app/AppShell.tsx` (`framed = useMediaQuery('(min-width: 600px)')`, `DemoPanel`, `StatusBar`), `app.css`.
- **Evidence:** screenshots `exports/audit/home-1280.png`, `public-split-1280.png`. The panel shows "Demo accounts: Abraham 0801 000 0001 … Demo PIN 1357" and "Closed sandbox beta". Only the sandbox tag is conditional on `config.sandbox`; the panel and frame are not gated on `deployEnv`. On public share pages the PACT brand row sits on top of the fake 9:41 status bar (`.al__brand` has no offset inside `.proto--framed`).
- **Impact:** people opening a WhatsApp link on a laptop (WhatsApp Web, iPad, landscape phone) see demo credentials for accounts that do not exist and a broken header. It reads as unfinished and it tells strangers a PIN. For a product asking for trust with money-adjacent data this is the first thing a desktop user sees.
- **Fix:** gate `DemoPanel` on `deployEnv !== 'production'` (and arguably on a `demo` flag); keep the frame but fix the offset for `.al__brand`, or drop the frame below ~900 px and render a centred 440 px column on desktop. Smallest: gate the panel, add `margin-top` for the brand row inside `.proto--framed`.
- **Gate:** public sandbox beta. Trusted beta: tell testers to use phones.

## E. P2 findings

**Correctness and data**
- **IDEM-1: V2 creates are not idempotent** (`app.ts` routes for `POST /circles`, `/circles/:id/asks|plans|splits`, task creation). `idempotent()` wraps only money-moving Pact routes. A retried POST on a flaky connection creates a second Plan/Split/Ask and sends a second push and in-app notification to everyone. Fix: wrap the five create routes the same way (the client already sends `Idempotency-Key`). Also `idempotent()` fingerprints with `JSON.stringify(body, Object.keys(body).sort())`, whose replacer array drops nested keys not also at top level, so different nested bodies can share a hash (app.ts:224).
- **OG-1: `$` sequences in titles corrupt shared-link HTML** (`server/src/lib/og.ts`). `injectOg` passes a *string* replacement to `String.replace` for `<title>` and for the `twitter:card` insertion, so a title containing `$'`, `$&` or `` $` `` expands to chunks of the page. Reproduced: a title `Lunch $' and $& done` turns a 314-byte shell into 959 bytes with the app script tag four times. Not attacker-controlled markup (the pulled text is the page's own), but any Circle member can break a link preview and make the page boot twice. Fix: use function replacers (two lines) and add a test.
- **MEMB-1: Removal from a Circle is not sticky** (`circles.ts` `joinByToken`, ~line 250). A removed member (`status='left'`) rejoins with the same invite link; the owner must reset the link and tell everyone. Also removed members keep their Plan RSVPs, Split shares and any share links they hold. Fix: record `removed` distinct from `left` and refuse rejoin by link; decide what happens to their open Split shares.
- **LINK-1: Split links cannot be revoked or reset** (no `split_revoked_links`, no route; Ask and Plan have `share/reset`). A link posted in the wrong chat stays valid; it shows title, total, payer's first name and lets the holder settle their own share only. Cancelling the Split is the only exit. Fix: mirror Plan's reset (one table, one route).
- **STATE-1: Pact deadline job and cancel do not tell Home** (see LIFE-1). Also `completePact` only completes a Plan from `planning`/`confirmed`; fine.
- **ORG-1: Removed or closed organiser leaves Plans/Splits without an editor.** `created_by` is the only editor; no transfer. Low frequency, high confusion.

**Product coherence**
- **IA-1: The Activity tab is Pact-only** (`ActivityScreen.tsx` uses `useActivity` = `GET /activity` = `pacts.feed`). Home's "Recent → See all" and the nav tab promise cross-object activity; Circle/Ask/Plan/Split events are invisible there. Fix: either point See all at notifications, or extend the feed (the Home `recent` query is already the right shape).
- **IA-2: Back is hard-coded** (`AskScreen:66`, `PlanScreen`, `SplitScreen` use `backTo={/app/circles/:id}`; `PactDetailScreen` uses `/app/home`). Opening a Plan from Home's Needs You and pressing back lands on the Circle, not Home. The screens already receive `?from=home|circle`; use it.
- **IA-3: Starting anything needs a Circle first, and the detour loses typing.** `CreatePlanScreen`/`CreateSplitScreen`/`CreateAskScreen`: with zero Circles the circle step says "Create one first" and links away; the half-typed title is lost. A new user's first tap on "+" hits this.
- **COPY-1: Stale positioning everywhere outside the V2 screens.** `index.html`: `<title>PACT · Money works better together</title>`, description "create a shared money goal…", og:description "The people, money, tasks and deadline for a group plan"; `manifest.webmanifest` description; `OnboardingScreen`/`GuidedStartScreen`/`HomeFirstTime` (steps "Invite your people… Pay for the plan from the Pact") mention none of Circles, Asks, Plans, Splits (grep finds no such words in `src/features/onboarding`); the landing page only mentions them in one mockup.
- **HOME-1: Organizer next steps disappeared from Home.** The old Home showed `attentionFor(pact)` (invite people, "Everything looks handled, complete the Pact", pay the next line). Home V2 server rules cover only approvals, tasks you hold and contributions. Intentional and noted, but an organiser of a funded Pact no longer gets a prompt to execute or complete. Needs a product decision, not necessarily a fix.
- **HOME-2: Home does not refresh after the actions that clear its cards.** `homeKeys.all` is never invalidated by any mutation (grep). With `staleTime 10 s`, RSVP/vote/settle then back to Home shows the old card until the next fetch. This is the "did it work?" gap. Fix: invalidate `['home']` in the shared `useRefresh` helpers of asks/plans/splits and Pact mutations.
- **NOTIF-1: Fan-out with no ceiling.** New Ask pushes everyone in the Circle (`asks.ts:327`), new Plan the same (`plans.ts:299`), Split pushes each person generically. Caps are per-object (30 Plans, 20 Asks, 50 Splits per Circle) but nothing limits how many a single member can create per hour. A member can push-spam a 30-person Circle in a minute. Fix: per-user creation rate limit (e.g. 10 per 10 minutes) and grouping.

**Security hygiene**
- **SEC-2: SMS OTP is the only factor for signing in** (`auth.ts verifyOtp`). The PIN protects money movement, not sign-in. A SIM-swapped phone gets the whole account's data (Splits, Circles). Fine for sandbox; a real-money gate.
- **SEC-3: OTP quota can be burned by a third party** (`issueOtp`: 4 codes per phone per 15 min, no proof of ownership). Anyone can lock a victim out of sign-in for 15 minutes by requesting codes for their number. Low value to an attacker, annoying in a small friend group where numbers are known.
- **SEC-4: Pact invite codes are 8 characters of a 31-symbol alphabet (about 40 bits)** (`pacts.ts:571`), versus 256 bits for V2 tokens. Preview is rate limited at 60 per minute per IP, so online guessing is slow but not impossible with many IPs. Fine now; give them more length before wider distribution.
- **SEC-5: No test pins the security headers** (CSP, HSTS, frame-ancestors, referrer-policy). Helmet's defaults are likely correct (referrer `no-referrer`, so tokens do not leak by Referer) but I could not find a test and did not capture a live response; treat as unverified.

**Performance**
- **PERF-1: One 1,075 kB main chunk (330 kB gzip)** containing every authenticated screen, GSAP and ScrollTrigger. On 4G this is the first-load cost for every shared-link visitor too, since `/a /p /s /r` render inside `AppShell`. Biggest win: `lazy()` the authenticated route groups (Pact detail, Create Pact, wallet, profile) and keep GSAP out of the shell (it is imported by `InstallPactPrompt`/`HoldButton`).
- **PERF-2: Polling.** Ask/Plan/Split screens poll every 8 s each; the Circle screen mounts three of them plus the Circle (20 s); Home polls `/home` (20 s), pacts, wallet, notifications. A phone left open on a Circle makes about 20 requests per minute. Use `refetchIntervalInBackground: false` (default) but raise to 20-30 s, or poll only the visible screen's object.
- **PERF-3: `GET /home` runs 28 queries** (measured; constant in the number of items, 91 ms locally). Acceptable now; it is polled every 20 s per open Home. Not N+1.

**Accessibility**
- **A11Y-1: All font sizes are px** (121 declarations, zero rem). Browser/OS "larger text" settings that change the default font size will not scale the UI, only page zoom will. Move the type tokens to rem.
- axe (WCAG 2 A/AA) is clean on Home, create flows, Split/Plan/Recap pages and the share pages, in light and dark. 20 of the 38 CSS files that animate have a `prefers-reduced-motion` rule, plus `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`; I did not check the other 18 individually.

**Testing**
- **TEST-1:** no concurrency tests (two people settling the last two shares at once, double vote, double conversion); no browser E2E for V2 flows (`scripts/e2e.mjs` is the old Pact flow; my QA scripts were deleted); no test for auth-return on `/p /s /a /r`; no security-header test; no test that closing an account cleans V2 data (it does not).

## G. UX journey findings

**Four friends, Ghana (new user → Circle → Ask → Plan → Pact → completion).**
1. First launch: onboarding explains Pacts only (COPY-1), then Home. The `+` offers Ask / Plan / Split / Pact and the first three need a Circle, which they have not made (IA-3).
2. Circle creation and invite are short and good; the Circle page is social (emoji, avatars, live line), not administrative. Invite link has no expiry and is reusable by design.
3. Ask → share → vote: link to vote in two taps; auth returns to the same Ask (tested). The recipient sees the question and options immediately. Good.
4. Plan: "Are you in?" first, then Decisions, To do, People; "Make it a Pact" waits for momentum. Good. A Plan edit after RSVPs asks for confirmation. Good.
5. Make it a Pact: pre-fills the existing form; good. Then the Plan says "executing through its Pact" with an Open Pact button. If that Pact is cancelled, the Plan is stuck (LIFE-1).
6. Contribute / execute / complete: unchanged Pact flows. On completion the Plan becomes done and a recap appears on Home, but the Pact page itself has no "View recap" link.
7. After that, "start something else": the `+` works, but the Activity tab shows nothing about the Plan, Asks or Split (IA-1).

**Circle → Split → settlement.** Create is four steps and the review shows the maths clearly. The shared link shows total and payer, asks the visitor to sign in to see their share, returns them to the same Split. "Mark settled" says "This only records that it was settled outside PACT." Good. Friction: the payer cannot revoke the link (LINK-1); Split detail "More" only appears for the creator, so a participant who disagrees has no "something looks wrong" path.

## J. Performance findings

**Frontend.** Main chunk 1,075 kB / 330 kB gzip (PERF-1). CSS 196 kB / 30 kB gzip. Fonts: five Geist subsets (cyrillic/vietnamese are only fetched if used). No image-heavy screens. Polling cost in PERF-2. Home re-renders are cheap (lists capped at 6-12).

## 75. If 20 real groups started tomorrow

1. **They open a link on a laptop and see a fake phone with "Demo PIN 1357".** Many WhatsApp links get opened in WhatsApp Web. The shared Split page also has its header drawn over the fake clock. This is the first impression for the second person in every group (PROD-1).
2. **Retries and stale screens make things look wrong.** On a weak connection a double tap can create two Plans or two Splits and buzz everyone twice (IDEM-1); after voting or settling, Home still shows the card for several seconds (HOME-2). People will think the action failed and do it again.
3. **Nobody is told what the pieces are.** Onboarding, the app title and the manifest still describe a money goal; the first tap on `+` demands a Circle they have not made and drops what they typed (COPY-1, IA-3). Groups that never get past that never create the second thing.
4. **The group chat gets noisy, and the places to look are empty.** Every Ask and Plan pushes the whole Circle, one member can push-spam the rest (NOTIF-1), and the Activity tab shows none of it (IA-1). Back buttons dump people on the Circle instead of where they came from (IA-2).
5. **A cancelled Pact leaves a Plan that says it is still executing.** The first group that abandons a Pact gets a Plan with no actions and no way to cancel it (LIFE-1); and anyone who leaves or closes their account leaves Split debts and push subscriptions behind (DATA-1), which is exactly where trust is lost in a product about who owes whom.
